## Built to use while you work

Open a guide beside Salesforce and follow the numbered steps. Search by action, field, or error with **⌘K / Ctrl+K**. Each module has its own navigation, examples, and troubleshooting reference.

The guides use customer-free screenshots and labeled demonstration examples. They require no video or voice-over to follow. Salesforce access, division configuration, and agreement availability still determine what each user can do.
