# Practice the send checklist

Try a fictional agreement before working with a customer. Change the amounts, complete the missing details, and see why a Send button may still be unavailable.

<PracticeChecklist />

## Three things to try

1. Complete the signer’s address and the balance due date. Notice that review and approval are still needed.
2. Mark review complete. With matching amounts, the example becomes Ready to Send.
3. Change the Balance to $2,400. The mismatch blocks the example again, even though review was completed.

This exercise covers a small set of common blockers. In Salesforce, read the entire validation list and verify all agreement details.
