# Services & food and beverage

Select the services and elections actually included in the agreement. The options come from the division’s template.

## Services & Add-Ons

1. Open **Services & Add-Ons**.
2. Select each agreed service.
3. Complete the additional fields that appear for the selected service.
4. Wait for saving to finish before previewing or sending.
5. Review the selected services in the PDF.

For the Akron checklist:

| Service selection | Additional detail |
| --- | --- |
| Security Personnel | Required hours |
| EMT | Required hours |
| Videographer | Optional Additional Cameras count |
| Band/Live Music | Optional Electrical and A/V Needs |

Only selected services become agreement service records. The PDF can still show the full checklist with unselected boxes empty. Do not interpret an unchecked printed option as a purchased service.

Changes save automatically when required supporting values are complete. A selected service with missing required hours is not finished simply because its box is checked.

## Food & Beverage

1. Open **Food & Beverage**.
2. Choose the applicable template elections.
3. If **Cash Bar** or **Hosted Bar** is selected, enter **Number of Bartenders** as a whole number of at least one.
4. Enter **Outside F&B Fee** and **Fee Basis** when applicable and available.
5. Wait for the save, then review the PDF’s food-and-beverage exhibit.

Akron’s screen is focused on the agreement’s elections and fees. Menu development, staffing coordination, and the full operational event plan still need the division’s normal planning process.

## When a tab or option is missing

The division template controls these sections. Ask the administrator to confirm the template before assuming your permissions are wrong or selecting an unrelated option to work around it.
