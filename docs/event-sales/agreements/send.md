# Preview, review & send

Work through these steps in order. Previewing and approving do not email the signers; **Send via Adobe Sign** is the sending action.

## 1. Finish and save the agreement

Save Event Details explicitly. Let autosaves finish on services, food and beverage, access, and existing payment terms. Confirm the Company and Client signers.

Read the validation banner above the tabs. **Cannot send — … items need attention** lists the problems and links to the relevant correction. The Send button stays disabled while blockers remain.

## 2. Preview the PDF

Select **Preview PDF** in the header. For an editable agreement, Salesforce generates the preview and opens it. Once the agreement is locked, Preview PDF opens its current saved document.

Read the whole document, including:

- Correct legal customer name, event description, and Effective Date.
- Event Date, times, Time Zone, attendance, and every location.
- Approved access conditions and service selections.
- Food-and-beverage elections, bartender count, and applicable fees.
- Usage Fee, Deposit, Balance, and due dates.
- Onsite and legal-notice details.
- Company and Client signer names, titles, and email addresses.

If anything is wrong, close the preview, correct and save the agreement, and generate a new preview. A preview is a saved copy of the data at that moment.

## 3. Submit for Review

When the Draft is complete, select **Submit for Review** from the validation next-step button or **More agreement actions**. Status changes to **Internal Review**.

Follow your division’s authorization process for who reviews and approves the contract. The availability of an action is not a substitute for that business responsibility.

## 4. Approve for Send

Review the agreement and select **Approve for Send**. Salesforce rechecks the required details. When successful, status becomes **Ready to Send**.

Approval alone does not send email. If approval fails, use the specific validation messages to correct the record and recheck.

## 5. Send via Adobe Sign

1. Confirm the correct agreement and both recipients one last time.
2. Select **Send via Adobe Sign** once.
3. Wait for the result.
4. Review the current document and agreement status.
5. If processing is still underway, use **Refresh status** in Documents & Signatures (or **Sync Adobe Status** in More agreement actions) and check the same request.

The current document shows **Awaiting signatures** and the agreement becomes **Out for Signature** after Adobe confirms the active request. A queued action, generated PDF, or unsent Adobe authoring copy is not the same confirmation.

## If sending fails

Read the persistent error. Keep the existing signature request available for reconciliation, and use Sync Adobe Status where available. Do not create another agreement or repeatedly click Send to bypass an unclear outcome.

For connection, authoring, or repeated send failures, provide the administrator with the agreement reference, exact error, time, and current status. See [Troubleshooting](../reference/troubleshooting).
