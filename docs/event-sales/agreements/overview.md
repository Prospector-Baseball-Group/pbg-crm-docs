# Agreement workflow

Build the customer-facing contract from an Event Sales Opportunity. The detailed workspace in this guide uses the active **Akron Special Event Agreement** template.

<div class="step-strip"><span>Draft</span><span>Internal Review</span><span>Ready to Send</span><span>Out for Signature</span><span>Executed</span></div>

## The route to a signed agreement

| Step | What you do | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create | Open the opportunity’s Special Event Agreements card | Correct account, event date, locations, and template |
| Complete | Work through the agreement tabs | Customer, onsite and notice contacts, services, access, food and beverage |
| Reconcile | Complete Scheduled Payments | Exactly one Deposit and one Usage Fee Balance; their total equals Usage Fee |
| Confirm signers | Review Documents & Signatures | Correct Company and Client signers with complete details |
| Preview | Generate and read the PDF | Document reflects the current saved agreement |
| Review and approve | Submit for Review, then Approve for Send | No remaining validation blockers |
| Send | Select Send via Adobe Sign | Adobe confirms the request is Out for Signature |
| Track | Review signer and document status | Completion, signed PDF, and audit trail |

## Learn the workspace tabs

| Tab | What belongs here |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | A summary of the event, customer, location, dates, attendance, and financial details |
| Event Details | Event information, legal customer name, onsite contact, legal-notice contact, and agreement financial values |
| Spaces & Access | Copied event locations and any additional approved access areas |
| Services & Add-Ons | The division template’s services checklist and required supporting details |
| Food & Beverage | Template-specific elections, bartender count when applicable, and fee details |
| Scheduled Payments | Payment types, amounts, due dates, and available receipt fields |
| Documents & Signatures | Signer selection, document preview, and signature tracking |
| Activity | Agreement tasks and follow-up |

Spaces & Access, Services & Add-Ons, and Food & Beverage are controlled by the division template. A different template can expose a different set of tabs or fields.

## Three records, three responsibilities

The **opportunity** is your sales and booking record. The **agreement** is the contract workspace. A **document copy** is the PDF prepared from the saved agreement details. Previewing, approving, sending, and signing are distinct actions.

Editing the opportunity does not continuously rewrite the agreement. An editable Draft can be refreshed deliberately. Sent document copies preserve the information used when they were prepared.
