# Event details & contacts

Use **Event Details** to turn the opportunity’s sales information into accurate agreement terms.

## Complete and save Event Details

1. Open **Event Details**.
2. Review Event Description, Effective Date, Event Date, start time, end time, Time Zone, attendance, and Primary Event Space.
3. Confirm the **Client Legal Entity Name**.
4. Review **Onsite Contact** and **Legal Notice Contact** separately.
5. Enter the agreement’s financial details, including Usage Fee and applicable deposit and balance-date values.
6. Select **Save Event Details** and confirm the save succeeds.

This tab has an explicit Save action. Other tabs use autosave; do not assume that simply leaving Event Details commits your edits.

## Give each contact role the right person

| Role | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Primary Contact on the opportunity | Main customer relationship for the sale; used to seed agreement information |
| Onsite Contact | Person coordinating the event onsite, with name, phone, and email |
| Legal Notice Contact | Recipient and address for formal notices under the agreement |
| Client Signer | Person authorized to sign for the customer |
| Company Signer | Authorized PBG/venue user who signs for the company |

One person may fill several roles, but verify each role rather than assuming they are interchangeable.

## Check snapshot fields after changing a lookup

The onsite name, phone, and email are copied values. Changing the onsite contact lookup directly does not automatically guarantee those adjacent fields are repopulated. Review and update the displayed details, or use the appropriate Draft refresh from the opportunity.

The Client Signer’s required details are checked against the selected person’s record. Use the agreement’s signer edit action to correct that person. Changing only the Legal Notice Contact does not fix a missing Client Signer address.

## Keep the financial fields consistent

Usage Fee is the amount against which the core payment schedule is validated. Do not stop after changing a header amount: open **Scheduled Payments** and make sure the Deposit and Usage Fee Balance rows still agree with it.
