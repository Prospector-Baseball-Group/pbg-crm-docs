# Book a multi-day event

Use **one Event Sales opportunity** for a booking that reserves the same locations continuously across consecutive days. Keep one agreement and one set of event totals.

## Enter the reservation window

1. Create or open the Event Sales opportunity.
2. Enter **Event Start Date** and **Event Start Time** for the beginning of the reservation.
3. Enter **Event End Date** and **Event End Time** for the end. Leave End Date blank for a single-day event.
4. Include setup and teardown in this window. Enter the attendee programme or daily opening hours in **Event Requirements**.
5. Select every occupied location, choose the primary location, and save.
6. Review the [calendar](./calendar), then save the appropriate hold or booking status.

Times are local to the event location. For Akron and Jacksonville, use Eastern Time. In an agreement, retain the venue's **America/New_York** time zone rather than changing times to match your own computer's zone.

The end date/time must be later than the start. For example, an event can start at 6 PM on one day and finish at 9 AM the next day.

## Understand continuous occupancy

Every selected location is reserved from the first start time through the final end time, **including overnight and any breaks**. Another held or booked event cannot overlap that interval in the same selected location. A booking can start exactly when the previous booking ends; include turnaround time in your reservation if needed.

Selecting a location named Stadium or Full Ballpark Rental does not automatically select every room or club. Select every location that needs protection and confirm venue-wide availability with the booking owner.

### Two-day example

A fictional workshop reserves a club from **November 12, 8 AM through November 13, 6 PM**. Sessions run 9 AM–5 PM each day, and equipment stays overnight.

- Start Date: November 12; Start Time: 8 AM.
- End Date: November 13; End Time: 6 PM.
- Event Requirements: “Workshops 9 AM–5 PM each day; equipment remains overnight.”
- The calendar shows the booking on both dates, with one entry in List view.
- The venue stays reserved overnight.
- The Opportunity Amount and agreement fees are totals for the booking; entering a second date does not multiply them.

An end time of midnight finishes the reservation at the beginning of the end date. That final date is not shown as an occupied day in Month view.

## Check the agreement

Create the agreement from the Opportunity's **Create Agreement** action or its Special Event Agreements section. The agreement copies both dates and the times. Review the range in **Event Details** and in the PDF preview before sending.

If the booking changes, update the Opportunity first. For an editable Draft agreement, use **Refresh from Opportunity**, then review the copied details and payment schedule. Editing agreement dates alone does not update the venue reservation. Existing document snapshots retain their original dates; sent or executed agreements require the established correction process.

Payment terms and deadlines still apply to the whole event. End Date does not create daily charges, change deposit rules, or move existing due dates automatically.

## Dates with gaps or different venues

This workflow does not select scattered dates or release a venue between daily sessions. Do not enter a week-long range for two separate dates unless the venue really is reserved throughout. Ask the division's booking owner to plan those exceptions before confirming availability; do not duplicate the full sales value into daily opportunities.
