# Log activity & follow up

Leave the record ready for the next person to act: what happened, what happens next, who owns it, and when it is due.

## Log the interaction on the opportunity

1. Open the Event Sales Opportunity.
2. Use the activity composer or available **Log a Call**, **New Task**, or email action.
3. Enter a concise subject and useful notes: the customer’s request, decisions, and commitments.
4. Confirm **Related To** points to this opportunity and select the relevant person where available.
5. Save the activity.

Logging the interaction on the opportunity keeps it with the specific event. A note on the account alone can be harder to connect to the right booking.

## Set the next action

Update **Next Step** with a concrete action, such as “Send revised room layout after attendance confirmation.” Set **Follow-Up By** and create a task for the person responsible when an assigned reminder is needed.

For a task, verify the assigned owner, due date, subject, status, and related record. Complete it after doing the work, then create the next action if the sale remains open.

## Work the daily queue

1. Open Home and choose **My Data**.
2. Review **My Event Sales Tasks** and open urgent items.
3. Use **View all tasks** for the full list and verify its filters.
4. Review open opportunities for missing or stale next steps.
5. Switch to Team Data when reviewing your division’s work.

## Know which tasks are automatic

| Trigger | Typical task |
| --- | --- |
| New opportunity where creation automation applies | Initial Outreach |
| Change to Quoted | Follow up on quote |
| Change to Walkthrough | Prepare for walkthrough |
| Change to Contract Sent | Follow up on contract |
| Start or change a tentative hold with an expiration | Review expiring event hold |

Manually created Akron opportunities intentionally do not receive the ordinary creation-time outreach task. Akron Eventective inquiries do. Later stage and hold changes have their own task rules. Do not assume a task will be created for every save.

## Use email templates deliberately

Open **Email Templates** from the app or Home. Choose content you can access, personalize it for the event, and check recipients, dates, amounts, and links in the actual email composer before sending. A template or task is not proof of delivery.
