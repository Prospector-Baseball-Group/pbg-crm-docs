## One workflow. A clear next step.

<div class="step-strip"><span>01 · Customer</span><span>02 · Opportunity</span><span>03 · Locations & hold</span><span>04 · Agreement</span><span>05 · Signature</span><span>06 · Handoff</span></div>

The **Opportunity** tracks the sale and the booking. The **Special Event Agreement** holds the customer-facing contract details. The **signature request** tracks whether that agreement has actually been sent and signed. This handbook shows you how to work across all three.

## A guide for the way PBG works

The core Event Sales workflow supports your division’s pipeline, locations, activities, and reporting. The detailed Special Event Agreement instructions currently describe the **Akron** template. Availability and options can differ by division and permissions.
