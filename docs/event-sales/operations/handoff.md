# Prepare the event handoff

A signed agreement is the beginning of event delivery. Give the operating team an accurate, usable plan.

## Confirm the booking

Check the Opportunity’s Stage and Booking Status against the actual signed outcome. Review all locations, Event Date, times, attendance, and the customer’s onsite contact.

The agreement’s Executed status is separate from these opportunity fields. Review them explicitly rather than assuming every sales or operational step is completed automatically.

## Assemble the handoff

| Include | Verify |
| --- | --- |
| Customer and onsite contact | Correct names and event-day phone numbers |
| Timing | Event date, start/end time, time zone, and approved access arrangements |
| Locations | All selected spaces and the primary location |
| Access | Approved additional areas and their conditions |
| Services | Selected services, staffing hours, equipment, and supporting details |
| Food and beverage | Contract elections plus the separate operational plan |
| Payments | Agreed amounts and dates; confirmed receipt information where available |
| Documents | The executed PDF and audit trail, with any authorized subsequent documentation |
| Open actions | Named owners, due dates, and outstanding customer decisions |

## Work the tasks

Use the opportunity’s and agreement’s Activity sections for follow-up. Assign each remaining task to a responsible person. A generated handoff task does not mean the handoff itself has taken place.

Division-specific fulfillment processes may add their own tasks or checklist. Follow those where applicable; the agreement does not replace the venue’s event-day operating process.

## If the event changes

Review the impact on location availability, staffing, catering, payment dates, customer commitments, and the signed agreement. Escalate contractual changes through the authorized correction process. Updating the opportunity alone does not amend an already signed PDF.
