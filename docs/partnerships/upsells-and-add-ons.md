# Upsells and add-ons

When an Akron sponsor expands a purchase, choose the **Type** on the **Partnership Opportunity** based on what the sponsor is buying. Choose **Upsell** when the sponsor upgrades an asset or package. Choose **Addon** (the Salesforce label for an add-on) when the sponsor keeps the original asset and buys a separate one alongside it.

## Same sponsor, two ways to expand

Suppose a sponsor is considering **Game Program - Half Page** in the Akron RubberDucks' 2027 inventory.

- **Upsell:** The sponsor switches to **Game Program - Full Page**. The full-page ad replaces the half-page ad and gives the sponsor twice the space in the program.
- **Add-on:** The sponsor keeps the half-page ad and buys **Website Link Placement**. Fans can now find the sponsor online as well as in print.

## Two more Akron examples

- **Upsell:** A sponsor moves from the **5-Game Suite Package** to the **10-Game Suite Package**. The larger package replaces the smaller one and gives the sponsor five more games to host guests.
- **Add-on:** A sponsor with a **Concourse Sign** adds **Concourse Table - Non-Firework** for one game. The sign stays in place, and the sponsor's staff can meet fans at the table.
