# Review the team pipeline

A productive review ends with clear owners and next actions. Use the numbers to find the records that need a conversation.

## Daily review

1. Confirm your scope. If available, **Team view** on Home shows accessible Ticketing records, which may span divisions.
2. Review overdue follow-ups and opportunities requiring attention.
3. Open the specific deal with the rep: what changed, what is the next decision, and when will it happen?
4. Confirm Stage, Close Date, Amount, Next Step, and Follow-Up By.
5. Use the Hustle Score **Full Team** tab as activity context, understanding its [scope and timing](hustle-score.md).

## Weekly review

- Review open pipeline by Stage, Division, and Season.
- Identify overdue close dates, missing next actions, and stalled conversations.
- Reconcile newly won sales against the appropriate ticket orders and agreement status.
- Review losses and the recorded objection or reason.
- Check target/credit attribution through the approved report rather than assuming Opportunity Owner and Quota Credit Rep are identical.

## Access and metrics are separate

The **Ticket Sales Home Manager** permission enables the team-view control. It does not itself grant record access. Record sharing determines which deals the manager can view or edit.

Likewise, Hustle Score's Full Team activity view is not proof of unrestricted management access to those reps' opportunities. Ask an administrator to review the exact inaccessible record and the required action.

## Quota display

The homepage uses active Sales Target plans whose dates include today. Personal mode scopes target rows to the current target owner; team mode aggregates the accessible applicable target rows. Missing or inactive plans and limited access can leave quota unavailable.

A missing quota panel or “not active” state is not evidence that the rep's target is zero. Confirm the plan, dates, assignments, and reporting configuration.
