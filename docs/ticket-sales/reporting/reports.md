# Use reports and dashboards

Use the homepage for action and reports for a complete review. Always read the filters before interpreting a total.

## Open a report

1. Choose **Reports** from the Ticket Sales shortcuts or app navigation.
2. Search for the report by purpose or name, and open the appropriate shared folder.
3. Review **Show Me/owner scope**, record type, date field/range, Division, Season, and Stage filters.
4. Run or refresh the report.
5. Open a detail record to investigate an unexpected result.

Your folder and record permissions determine which reports and rows you can see. A shared report does not automatically grant access to all underlying customer records.

## Useful reporting questions

| Question | Check these dimensions |
| --- | --- |
| What is in my pipeline? | Ticketing record type, open Stage, owner, Amount |
| Who needs follow-up? | Follow-Up By, Next Step, owner, open Stage |
| What closed this period? | Closed Won, the report's Close Date range, Season, Division |
| How much activity was completed? | Closed Tasks, activity type, assignee, the activity date definition |
| Why does my quota differ? | Active plan dates, target owner, credit-rep rules, refresh timing |
| How much ticket revenue was earned? | The approved gross/net revenue definition and source-order reporting |

## Compare like with like

The most common reasons for different totals are a different record type, personal versus team scope, Season versus fiscal year, different date fields, Amount versus ticket revenue, or different refresh times.

A report including related contacts or line items can show several rows for one Task or Opportunity. Inspect the report's record grain and summary calculation before summing exported rows.

## Dashboards and the old homepage

Choose **Dashboards** to view the dashboards shared with you. **Old Homepage** on Ticket Sales Home opens the previous Ticketing dashboard. Check the dashboard's filters, running-user/viewing context, and refresh timestamp.

## Save your own view

When permissions allow, use **Save As** to create a personal variation rather than changing a shared team's report definition. Give it a clear name that includes its purpose and scope. Follow team policy before sharing or exporting customer data.
