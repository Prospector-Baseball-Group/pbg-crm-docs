# Ticket Sales

**Know your customer. Keep the next step clear. Bring the sale home.**

This is the working guide to PBG's **Ticketing** app in Salesforce. Start with the quick start if you are new, or jump to the job in front of you. The instructions cover the current PBG experience for Akron, Jacksonville, and Lancaster.

## A guide built around your day

**Getting started** explains the customer records and workspace. **Sales** takes a deal from first contact to a recorded outcome. **Agreements** walks through customer details, payments, preview, signature, and documents. **Customer service and reporting** help you follow through after the sale.

::: tip Which module should I use?
Use **Ticketing Opportunity** for ticket sales. Use the companion [**Event Sales** guide](/event-sales/) for special-event rentals and bookings. Partnership inventory, multi-year deals, and activation belong in the [Agilitek Partnerships guide](https://agilitek-solutions.gitbook.io/partnerships-crm-agilitek).
:::

## About the examples

The guide uses blank forms, an unsaved fictional activity example, an empty personal workspace, and previously captured **JG demo agreement** screenshots. Demo amounts and dates illustrate the controls; they are not pricing or payment policy. No customer records or private email conversations are published.
