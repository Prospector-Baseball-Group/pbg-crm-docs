# Your first 15 minutes

By the end of this walkthrough, you should know where to find a customer, create an Opportunity, record outreach, and leave a clear next step. Use a real approved sales prospect when you are ready to save; you can explore the forms and cancel without creating training records.

## 1. Open Ticketing

1. Sign in to your PBG Salesforce account.
2. Open the **App Launcher** (the nine-dot icon).
3. Search for **Ticketing** and select it.
4. Open **Home**. You should see **PBG TICKET SALES** above the welcome message.

The app is named **Ticketing**; the homepage calls the workspace **Ticket Sales**. They refer to the same module.

## 2. Find the customer before creating one

Use **Accounts** or Salesforce search. Search the customer's name, company, and email where available. Open an existing match and check its related contacts and opportunities.

- An individual buyer normally uses a **Person Account**.
- A company or organization uses a **Business Account**, with people stored as **Contacts**.

Follow [Accounts and contacts](accounts-contacts.md) if the relationship is missing or unclear.

## 3. Create the sales opportunity

From Home, choose **New Ticket Opportunity**. Confirm **Ticketing Opportunity**, then enter the customer, Division, Season, Stage, and expected Close Date. Add the Ticket Category, amount, and a useful Next Step. The [creation guide](../sales/create-opportunity.md) explains each field.

**Example next step:** “Call the group organizer Thursday to confirm headcount and game preference.” Set **Follow-Up By** to Thursday.

## 4. Log what happened

Open the Opportunity and choose **Log Task**. Record the Activity Type, subject, contacts, and notes. Use **Completed** for work that has happened. When the form includes Opportunity follow-up fields, fill **both** the next step and follow-up date.

After saving, verify the activity appears on the record and the Opportunity's next step is correct. A completed call and a future follow-up are different pieces of information.

## 5. Return to Home

Check **Opportunity Follow-Ups** and **Opportunities Requiring Attention**. The homepage uses the Opportunity's follow-up date and next step. Refresh after making changes.

## Before you finish the day

- Every active deal has an honest Stage and expected Close Date.
- Every open deal has a specific Next Step and Follow-Up By date.
- Today's completed work has been logged once and related to the right record.
- Won and lost deals have the correct outcome and closing notes.
