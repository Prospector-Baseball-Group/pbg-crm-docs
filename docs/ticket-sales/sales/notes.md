# Keep customer context in Notes

Use **Notes** for information you want to find again on a customer or individual: preferences, buying background, decision-making roles, or useful handoff context.

Notes are available on **Business Accounts, Person Accounts, and Contacts** in Ticketing, Event Sales, and Partnership. The record type determines where to find them.

## Choose the record and open Notes

| Information belongs to… | Open this record | Choose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A company, school, nonprofit, or other organization | Business Account | **Notes → New** |
| An individual represented by a Person Account | Person Account | **Notes → New** |
| An individual represented by a Contact | Contact | **Related → Notes → New** |

For example, put an organization's group-outing preferences on its Business Account. Put a particular coordinator's preferences on that person's Contact or Person Account. Check the record name before creating the note.

## Create and save a note

1. Open the intended customer or individual record.
2. Follow the path above and choose **New** in the Notes section.
3. Enter a descriptive **Title**, such as “Group outing preferences.”
4. Enter the note in **Compose text**. Use paragraphs or bullets to make it easy to scan.
5. Check **Related to** in the note editor. It should show the record you intended.
6. Choose **Done** and allow **Saving…** to finish.
7. Confirm the note appears in the Notes list, then select its title to reopen it and check the saved content.

## Find or update an existing note

Return to the same Account's **Notes** tab or the Contact's **Related → Notes** section. Select the note title to read it. If you have edit access, make the change and choose **Done**. Use **View All** when the related list does not show every note.

A note belongs to the record it is linked to. Opening that same record in Ticketing, Event Sales, or Partnership lets you find the same note; there is no need to create a separate copy for each app. Access to a record or note still depends on your permissions and sharing.

## Notes, activities, and follow-up

| What you need to record | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Lasting customer or individual context | **Notes** on the relevant Account or Contact |
| A completed call, meeting, or other interaction | **Log Task**, with the correct Activity Type, people, and Related To |
| Work someone needs to do later | A **Task** with an owner and due date |
| The next action on the sale | **Next Step** and **Follow-Up By** on the Opportunity |

A Note does not create a follow-up task and does not count as a completed Task in **Hustle Score**. Follow [Log activities](./log-activities.md) for outreach and [Future tasks & reminders](./future-tasks.md) for work still to do.

## If Notes is missing or a note cannot be found

- On an Account, look for the **Notes** tab. On a Contact, open **Related** and find the **Notes** section.
- Confirm you opened the intended record. A Business Account and a related individual are different records.
- If the page was open before the Notes update, refresh it and reopen the record.
- If Notes, **New**, or a saved note is still missing, ask your administrator to check the page assignment and your record/note access. Share the app name, record type, and record link through your internal support channel.
