# Log calls, meetings, and tasks

Log the work once, on the record that explains it. Choose the correct Activity Type and people so the history and activity reporting are useful.

## Choose where to start

| Conversation | Recommended starting record |
| --- | --- |
| A particular ticket sale or renewal | The Ticketing Opportunity |
| The broader relationship with an organization | The Business Account |
| A conversation with a specific person | The Contact or Person Account, with Related To checked |

The **Log Task** button may be in the record header or action dropdown. On Business Accounts, it is a custom action near the account name. Do not assume it must be inside the Activity tab.

## Record completed outreach

1. Open the record and choose **Log Task**.
2. Enter a short, useful **Subject**.
3. Choose the actual **Activity Type**: Call (Outbound), Call (Inbound), Email (Outbound), Email (Inbound), Meeting, SMS / Text, or LinkedIn Message.
4. Confirm **Assigned To**, date, and **Status**. Use Completed for work that has happened.
5. Select the people in **Name / Contacts** or **Name** and verify **Related To**.
6. Add notes: what was discussed, the outcome, and the promised next action.
7. On an Opportunity, fill both Opportunity follow-up fields if offered.
8. Save, then check the activity timeline and the Opportunity's Next Step.

The current general Log Task action defaults to Completed, Call (Outbound), and today. Change those defaults if they do not describe the activity. **New Task** is the better starting point for future work; check that its status remains open. Follow [Schedule and find future tasks](future-tasks.md) if work several months away disappears after saving.

## Business Account contact picker

The custom Business Account form prioritizes related contacts and allows broader search. Selected contacts appear as removable pills. You can select multiple contacts, up to 50; the first selected contact is the primary Name for the Task. Removing that person promotes the next selection.

Use **New Contact** only after searching. Creating a Contact is a separate save: the new person is linked to the Business Account and selected for the Task. Review the Task before saving it.

The Business Account stays in **Related To**. Choosing a person identifies who took part; it does not turn the activity into an Opportunity-specific follow-up.

## Avoid double-counting

If a call or email is already captured by an integration, inspect that activity before logging another copy. A Task with several associated contacts is still one Task; reports with relationship detail can display more than one row for it.

## Verify the result

Check the activity under the intended record. Expand it to confirm subject, type, status, assignee, contacts, and notes. If it is absent, review timeline filters and Related To before creating a second copy.

An account relationship alone does not make every activity appear on every associated business. Check the saved Task's Name/Contacts and Related To. For lasting reference information, use **Notes** on a Business Account or Person Account, or **Related → Notes** on a Contact. Follow [the Notes walkthrough](./notes.md); Notes do not count as completed Tasks.
