# Email, templates, and campaigns

Use Salesforce to preserve the customer context around outreach. Follow your team's approved communication and consent practices for sending email.

## Send a one-to-one email

1. Open the customer or Opportunity you are working.
2. Choose the available **Email** action.
3. Verify the recipient and sender address.
4. Insert an approved template if useful, then check every merged name, date, amount, and link.
5. Review the complete message before sending.
6. Check the record's activity history afterward and set the next follow-up on the Opportunity.

Sending from an Opportunity keeps the deal context close to the conversation. Selecting a Contact or Account alone does not establish the next Opportunity action.

## Find an email template

Use **Home → Email Templates** or the app navigation menu. Search the templates you can access and confirm the template is appropriate for your team and offering. If a template is missing, check folder access with your administrator.

## Understand captured email

Mailbox connections and Salesforce email features affect what appears on a timeline and what becomes a reportable Task. An email visible in Outlook or Gmail is not, by itself, proof that it will count in Salesforce activity reporting.

If expected email is missing, check the account/contact address, the related record, timeline filters, and your connected-mailbox status. Send your administrator the record link, approximate send time, and symptom. Do not disconnect and reconnect services repeatedly to troubleshoot an isolated message.

**Inbound email is excluded from today's Hustle Score.** For outbound email, the saved activity type, closed status, and activity timestamp determine inclusion.

## Use campaigns as context

**Campaigns** organize outreach initiatives and audiences. Select the appropriate **Primary Campaign Source** on an Opportunity when it is known. Review existing Campaign Members and statuses before adding or changing them.

Adding a person to a Campaign does not, by itself, prove that an email was sent or a sales cadence started. Automated cadence configuration is an administrator workflow and is not part of the ordinary Ticket Sales interface.
