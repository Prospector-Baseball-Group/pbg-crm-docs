# Track your pipeline and follow-ups

The homepage's work queue is driven by **Follow-Up By** and **Next Step** on the Opportunity. Keeping these current makes it possible to hand off a deal without losing the next action.

## Work the queue

1. Open **Home → Opportunity Follow-Ups**.
2. Start with **Overdue** and open the Opportunity you will work.
3. Review the latest activity and the promised Next Step.
4. Complete the outreach, then [log the activity](log-activities.md).
5. Set the new next action and follow-up date. Update Stage, Amount, or Close Date if the conversation changed them.
6. Return to Home and refresh. Use **This Week** to plan today's and the next seven days' follow-ups.

A good Next Step is “Email the two game options to the organizer Friday.” “Follow up” alone does not explain what needs to happen.

## Keep three dates straight

| Date | Meaning | Where you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task Due Date | When assigned work is due | Tasks and open-task lists |
| Opportunity Follow-Up By | When the next deal action should happen | Homepage follow-up queue |
| Completed Date/Time or imported call time | When completed activity is counted | [Hustle Score](../reporting/hustle-score.md) |

Completing a Task does not by itself guarantee a new Opportunity follow-up date. On Opportunity **Log Task**, fill both **Opportunity Next Step** and **Opportunity Follow-Up By** when available. The active automation copies them to the related Opportunity when both are filled, then clears those transfer fields on the Task. Otherwise, edit the Opportunity directly.

## Clear “requiring attention”

The panel flags an open Ticketing Opportunity if any of these apply:

- Follow-Up By is blank or before today.
- Next Step is blank.
- No activity is recorded.
- Last Activity is older than 14 days.

The preview shows a primary reason. Fixing one item may reveal another. Use **Open full list** to inspect more records, then refresh after your changes.

## Use list views for a broader review

Open **Opportunities**, select a Ticketing list view, and review its owner, stage, season, and date filters. Use the list's search, column sorting, and available filters. Where Kanban is available, it can help review stage distribution; use the record's Edit action if a change needs additional required fields.

The homepage works with open Ticketing Opportunities across seasons. A report limited to one fiscal year or season can correctly show a different total.
