# Ticket categories and products

**Ticket Category** is the broad sales classification. **Ticket Product** is an optional, more specific offering. The available products depend on **Division**, and a selected product must match its category.

## Choose the offering

1. Select the Opportunity **Division**.
2. Choose **Ticket Category**.
3. If useful for this sale, choose a **Ticket Product** offered for that division.
4. Confirm the combination below, then save.

Ticket Product is optional. Ticket Category is required before Closed Won. Do not pick an unrelated product to get past a validation message. If you change Division or Ticket Category, review Ticket Product again.

## Category choices

Full Season, Half Season, Flex / Mini Plan, Group, Picnic, Individual, FanRally Family, FanRally Single, Corporate Season, Suite, Space Rental.

Some older material uses **Product** for Ticket Category, **Flex Pass** for Flex / Mini Plan, or **Single** for Individual. This guide uses the current screen labels.

## Common combinations

- **Standard Full Season → Full Season**.
- **Corporate Full Season → Corporate Season**.
- **Duck Club → Group**.
- **All-You-Can-Eat Picnic → Picnic**.
- **Nightly Suite → Suite**.

Half Season, FanRally Family, FanRally Single, and Space Rental are available categories but do not have a more-specific Ticket Product in the current mapping. Leave the optional product blank unless the configured choices change.

For a missing offering, ask the administrator to review the division/product configuration. The Partnerships inventory Product Creator is a different workflow.
